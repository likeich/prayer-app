Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Phronema (the “App”) is an Orthodox Christian prayer and liturgical reading application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Who we are

The App is provided by Kyle Eichlin (“we,” “us,” or “our”). You can reach us at kyle@ark.place.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in, we use Google Sign-In. Google provides us with your email address and basic profile information (name and profile image) so we can create and identify your account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your prayer data. When you are signed in, the prayer rules, prayer sessions, and scheduling preferences you create are stored on our backend (Supabase) so they can sync across your devices.
  • App settings. Preferences such as theme, calendar selection, text size, and notification times are stored locally on your device. For signed-in users, prayer scheduling preferences may also be synced to your account.
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or your device contacts.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and provide a personal account.
  • To save and sync your prayer rules and sessions across your devices.
  • To deliver the prayer, calendar, and reading features you request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — for account and prayer-data storage and sync. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy.
  • orthocal.info — the App requests Orthodox calendar readings and commemorations from this public service. These requests do not include your identity.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and prayer data for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at kyle@ark.place. Locally stored settings are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kyle@ark.place.
