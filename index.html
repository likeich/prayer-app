Pray without ceasing.

Phronema is an Orthodox Christian prayer and liturgical reading app for your daily rule, the church calendar, and the lives of the saints.

Open the Web app Download for Android

Today, at a glance

The day’s feast and fast, a verse to dwell on, your prayer rule to check off, upcoming live services, and Orthodox chant — gathered the moment you open the app.

The whole church year

Every feast, fast, and tone of the year on the Julian or Gregorian calendar. Tap any day for its appointed readings and the saints commemorated.

The Scriptures and the Fathers

Read the whole Bible and the writings of the Church Fathers. Tap any verse for the original Greek, a word-by-word breakdown, Strong’s definitions, and patristic commentary.

A prayer for every moment

Search a full library of Orthodox prayers, or filter by occasion and prayer book — morning and evening, repentance, thanksgiving, intercession, and more.
